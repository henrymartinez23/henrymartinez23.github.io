Shorts Autopilot

Shorts Autopilot is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel @Henryz-shorts to publish short animated educational videos (maths, probability, science and similar topics) to that channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel and to read view, like and comment counts for those videos. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
