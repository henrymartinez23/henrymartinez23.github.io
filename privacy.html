Privacy Policy: Shorts Autopilot

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Shorts Autopilot ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel @Henryz-shorts to upload their own videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos, with their title, description and tags, to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read basic statistics (views, likes, comment counts) of the owner's own videos.

The app does not access any other Google data, and it never accesses other people's accounts.

How the data is used and stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner's account. Video statistics are used only to decide which kinds of videos to make next. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred anywhere except to Google's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server.
